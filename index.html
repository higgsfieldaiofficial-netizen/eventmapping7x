The technology

How 3D Projection Mapping Works

A building is not a screen — so first we measure it, build its exact 3D twin, design the film on that twin, and then throw it back onto the real façade with high-lumen projectors, aligned to the centimetre.

01 · Scan

Venue Scan & 3D Mesh

The façade is measured and rebuilt as an exact digital 3D mesh — every arch and window becomes a canvas.

02 · Design

Film Built on the Mesh

Your story film is designed frame-by-frame on that mesh, so visuals hug the real architecture.

03 · Align

Projectors Aligned & Blended

High-lumen projectors are positioned, warped and edge-blended until the film sits perfectly on the building.

04 · Show

The Live Moment

On cue, the palace itself performs — light, music and story in one seamless cinematic show.

Quick Answers

How does booking work?

Share your venue and dates → we design a concept and quotation → technical survey of the venue → setup and rehearsal → the live show on your day.

Do you travel for destination weddings?

Yes — we execute across India and at destination venues including Udaipur, Jaipur, Goa and Dubai, with travelling crew and compact projector kits.

Do we need to change our DJ or sound vendor?

No. Projection mapping is a pure visual spectacle — it integrates with your existing DJ, sound, décor and planning teams. No upsell, ever.

Is every show really custom-made?

Yes. No templates — content is built for your venue's architecture, your story and your music cues, then rehearsed on site before the event.
